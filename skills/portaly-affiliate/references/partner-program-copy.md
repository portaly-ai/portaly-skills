Ready-to-adapt 正體中文 text explaining the program to buyers. The creator owns the wording; what must survive editing is the **withdrawal requirements** section — that is the part that turns into their support burden if it goes missing.

Replace `{商品名稱}`, `{比例}` and `{金額}` with real values read from the promotion API. Never leave a placeholder in published copy.

## Full version — for a dedicated page

> ## 推薦夥伴計畫
>
> ### 怎麼參加
> 購買「{商品名稱}」之後，付款完成頁會提供你一條專屬推廣連結。不需要另外申請，也不需要先註冊任何帳號。
>
> ### 怎麼算
> 有人透過你的連結完成購買，你就能獲得成交金額的 {比例}%，也就是每成交一筆約 NT${金額}。
>
> 計算方式有兩點要先知道：
> - **以最後點擊為準**：如果對方先點了別人的連結、之後才點你的，這筆算你的；反過來也一樣。
> - **效期三天**：對方點過你的連結之後三天內完成購買才算。
>
> ### 什麼時候拿得到
> 分潤不會立刻變成可提領的錢。訂單需要經過一段觀察期，而且**如果該筆訂單退款，對應的分潤會被扣回**。實際狀態以 Portaly 後台顯示為準。
>
> ### 領取的條件
> 累積分潤本身沒有任何門檻，但要把錢領出來，你需要：
>
> 1. 一個 Portaly 帳號（用你購買時的同一個 email 註冊）
> 2. 台灣身分證
> 3. 本人名下的台灣銀行帳戶（戶名需與身分證相符）
>
> 提領時會有手續費與稅務代扣，實際金額以 Portaly 後台為準。
>
> 如果你不具備上述條件，你仍然可以拿到連結、也會累積分潤，但**目前無法提領**。請先確認再決定要不要花時間推廣。
>
> ### 查看成效與提領
> 到 [Portaly Rewards](https://rewards.portaly.cc) 用你的 Portaly 帳號登入。

## What not to write

- **Don't promise a payout date.** Settlement runs on Portaly's schedule and there is a withdrawal window; a specific date will be wrong.
- **Don't state fee or tax amounts.** They depend on the promoter's own situation and on thresholds that change. Point at the dashboard.
- **Don't imply overseas promoters can participate fully.** They can collect a link and accrue commission, but they cannot withdraw. Saying so up front is kinder than letting them find out later.
- **Don't show an earnings figure the creator's own site calculated.** Every number shown to a promoter should come from Portaly, or not be shown at all.

## English

No English version is provided on purpose: the program is limited to Taiwan-based Portaly accounts and payouts require a Taiwanese ID and bank account, so English-facing copy would advertise something most of its readers cannot complete. If the creator has English-speaking buyers who are Taiwan residents, translate the full version and keep the withdrawal requirements verbatim.
